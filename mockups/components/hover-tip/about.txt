# Each state's cell stands in for the window the tip lives in: the tip is kept inside it,
# above its target where there is room.
description: The small tooltip over a control, kept inside the window
state: short       A short tip above its button
state: wrapped     A long tip at its widest (260 px): on more lines
state: below       No room above the button (at the window's top): the tip under it
state: edge        The button at the window's left edge: the tip kept 4 px inside
state: link-rect   Pointing at a part of a line (an address in a text), not the whole text
