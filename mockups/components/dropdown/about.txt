# Each state's cell is a settings column (180 px wide dropdowns); a closed state shows a
# plain choice and one with a colour dot, an open one its list under the button.
description: A choice shown as a button, its options in a list that floats over the page
family: choices
state: closed           The current choice, plain and with its colour dot
state: placeholder      Nothing chosen yet: the placeholder, dimmed
state: hover            Under the pointer: the border lighter
state: focus            With the keyboard: the keyboard's ring around it
state: open             Open: the accent border, the list under it, the picked option tinted, its ring inside
state: groups           Options under their groups' titles, with colour dots
state: disabled-option  An option that cannot be picked: dimmed
state: current          The keys moved off the picked option: the keyboard's ring on theirs, the tint on the picked
state: long-label       A long choice: cut with …, plain and with its dot
