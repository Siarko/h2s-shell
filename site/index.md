# h2s-shell

A desktop shell for Hyprland, built with [Quickshell](https://quickshell.org) (Qt 6, QML):
a bar on every monitor with popups, notifications, on-screen toasts, a lock screen and a
settings window. It runs entirely on your own computer.

## Google Calendar

The shell can show your Google Calendar in the clock's popup — a month view, and events
you can add, edit and delete. It talks to Google directly from your computer; nothing goes
through a server of ours. See the [privacy policy](privacy.md).
